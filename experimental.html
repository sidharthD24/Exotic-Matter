Experimental investigations of hydrogen plasma, negative hydrogen ion sources, beam extraction, transport and optical diagnostics.

EXPERIMENTAL APPROACH

Understanding beam physics through measurement.

My experimental work focuses on the generation, extraction and characterization of negative hydrogen ion beams. Measurements of plasma and beam properties are used to investigate beam formation, spatial non-uniformity and transport behaviour.

Optical diagnostics, image-based measurements and tomographic techniques provide spatial information that can subsequently be correlated with computational models of plasma and beam transport.

01 — HYDROGEN PLASMA

Plasma generation and characterization.

Hydrogen plasma experiments

Experimental investigations are carried out on hydrogen plasma systems relevant to negative-ion production and beam extraction.

Plasma conditions are characterized to understand their influence on negative hydrogen ion production, extraction and subsequent beam behaviour.

  • Hydrogen plasma generation
  • Plasma parameter measurements
  • Electron temperature characterization
  • Investigation of plasma conditions for H⁻ production

02 — NEGATIVE ION SOURCE

Negative hydrogen ion extraction.

H⁻ beam extraction

Experimental studies investigate the extraction and acceleration of negative hydrogen ions from multi-aperture accelerator systems.

The extracted beam is studied under different operating conditions to understand the relationship between source parameters, extraction conditions and the resulting beam characteristics.

  • Negative hydrogen ion production
  • Multi-grid beam extraction
  • Beam current and operating conditions
  • Beam transport studies

03 — BEAM DIAGNOSTICS

Visualizing the extracted beam.

Visible-range CCD diagnostics

Visible-range CCD imaging is used to obtain spatial profiles of the extracted negative hydrogen ion beam.

The measured profiles provide information about beam intensity distribution, spatial inhomogeneity and the evolution of the beam along the transport line.

These experimental measurements also provide reference data for comparison with computational beam-transport and space-charge models.

04 — OPTICAL TOMOGRAPHY

Reconstructing the internal structure.

Plasma optical tomography

An optical tomography system is being developed to reconstruct spatial variations in photon-emitting plasma from multiple line-of-sight measurements.

The diagnostic combines multiple CCD cameras, pinhole optics and computational reconstruction techniques to obtain two-dimensional information about the emitting region.

  • Multi-camera optical diagnostics
  • Pinhole imaging
  • Line-of-sight measurements
  • Back-projection and tomographic reconstruction

EXPERIMENT × SIMULATION

Measurements provide the physical reference for simulation.

Experimental beam profiles and plasma measurements are used to investigate the physical mechanisms governing beam transport. Computational modelling provides a complementary approach for examining effects such as space charge, beam divergence and electromagnetic interactions.
